# Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

This book provides a comprehensive guide to the exciting and rapidly evolving fields of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. Over four modules, we will explore the foundational concepts, cutting-edge technologies, and practical applications that are shaping the future of intelligent robots.

## Modules

### Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)

Learn about the Robot Operating System 2 (ROS 2), its core concepts, Python integration, robot modeling with URDF, and sensor integration.

### Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo + Unity)

Dive into robot simulation with Gazebo and Unity, exploring digital twins, simulated sensors, and building realistic simulation environments.

### Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac)

Discover the NVIDIA Isaac platform, Isaac Sim for perception pipelines, Isaac ROS for accelerated perception, navigation with Nav2, and reinforcement learning for sim-to-real transfer.

### Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)

Explore how Vision-Language-Action pipelines enable humanoid robots to interact naturally with their environment through voice commands, cognitive planning, and multimodal interaction.

## About This Book

This book is designed for engineering students, researchers, and professionals interested in understanding and building the next generation of humanoid robots. It combines theoretical knowledge with practical insights, conceptual examples, and references to real-world robotics SDKs.
