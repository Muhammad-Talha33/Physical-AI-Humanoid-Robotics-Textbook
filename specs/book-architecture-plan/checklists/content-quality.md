## Content Quality Checklist: APA Citation Style

This checklist ensures that all content adheres to APA (7th edition) citation guidelines for academic rigor and consistency.

### General Citation Guidelines

- [ ] All direct quotes are properly enclosed in quotation marks and followed by an in-text citation (Author, Year, p./para. #).
- [ ] All paraphrased or summarized information from external sources is followed by an in-text citation (Author, Year).
- [ ] Every in-text citation has a corresponding entry in the References section.
- [ ] Personal communications (e.g., emails, interviews) are cited in-text but not included in the References list.
- [ ] Common knowledge or information widely available in multiple sources does not require citation.

### In-Text Citations

- [ ] **Author-Date Style:** All in-text citations follow the author-date format (e.g., Smith, 2020).
- [ ] **Multiple Authors:**
  - [ ] Two authors: (Author A & Author B, Year)
  - [ ] Three or more authors: (Author A et al., Year)
- [ ] **No Author:** Use the title of the work (or a shortened title) and the year.
- [ ] **No Date:** Use "n.d." for no date (e.g., Smith, n.d.).
- [ ] **Specific Parts:** Page numbers or paragraph numbers are included for direct quotes (e.g., Smith, 2020, p. 45) or (Smith, 2020, para. 3).

### References Section

- [ ] A dedicated "References" section is included at the end of each module or chapter where citations are used.
- [ ] Entries are alphabetized by the first author's last name.
- [ ] Each entry uses a hanging indent.
- [ ] **Books:**
  - [ ] Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work*. Publisher.
- [ ] **Journal Articles:**
  - [ ] Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical, volume*(issue), pages. DOI
- [ ] **Webpages:**
  - [ ] Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site name. URL
- [ ] **No Author/Date:** Adapt format as necessary, moving the title to the author position or using "n.d."

### Figures and Tables

- [ ] All figures and tables are numbered sequentially.
- [ ] Each figure and table has a clear, descriptive title.
- [ ] Sources for figures and tables are cited below the element if not original.

### Factual Verification and Plagiarism Checks

- [ ] All technical claims and data points are cross-referenced with at least two credible, independent sources (e.g., official documentation, peer-reviewed articles, established benchmarks).
- [ ] Any statistics, figures, or research findings presented are accompanied by citations to their original source.
- [ ] Paraphrased content accurately reflects the original source's meaning without direct copying or close paraphrasing that could be construed as plagiarism.
- [ ] Direct quotes are used sparingly and are always properly attributed and enclosed in quotation marks.
- [ ] AI-generated content (if any) is reviewed for factual accuracy and originality, and edited to ensure it aligns with human expertise and does not contain hallucinations.
- [ ] Tools for plagiarism detection (if available in the workflow) are utilized to ensure the originality of the content.

This checklist should be used by all content creators to ensure consistent and accurate APA citation throughout the "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics" book.
